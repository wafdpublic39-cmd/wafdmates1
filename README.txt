Wafd Mates website (static, for Vercel)
1. Upload this whole folder to Vercel (drag and drop, or push to GitHub and import).
2. index.html = the site, data.js = all people/gallery data, images/ = gallery pictures.
3. To add or change people or photos, edit data.js (instructions are at the top of that file) and re-deploy.
4. Contact form: put your email or a Formspree URL in data.js (CONFIG) so messages reach you.
